Lyon

France · 2022–2025 · Population 1.9 million

The challenge
A large and expanding network, with several operator contracts to bring together under a single brand and common service standards.
Our role
Developing the strategy to split the network into two lots – metro and tram on one side, buses on the other – and delivering the franchising scheme, including a publicly owned company to manage customer relations.
The outcome
The first competitive tender in France to be split into two separate packages, designed to encourage competition.
See it on a study visit →

Sélestat

France · 2022–2025 · Population 78,000

The challenge
Four rural districts needed to procure a single bus network jointly.
Our role
Developing the network, including new demand-responsive transport (DRT) services, and delivering the franchising scheme for the newly created joint authority.
The outcome
The first franchise let by a French joint authority of this kind, comparable to a UK combined authority. The contract was awarded to the incumbent operator.
See it on a study visit →
