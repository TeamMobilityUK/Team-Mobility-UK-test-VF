Founded by two public-sector consultancies

Team Mobility UK was set up by Trans-Missions (France) and KCW (Germany). Both work exclusively for public authorities, large and small, urban and rural, and bring 25 years of experience with franchising and municipal ownership across Europe.

Active in the UK bus community

Close links with the Bus Centre of Excellence and active members of its Bus Franchising Group. Speakers at Quality Bus and Quality Rural Bus conferences on franchising practice, DRT in France and free public transport in continental Europe.

What we do for LTAs

A custom-built team for each authority, flexible and responsive, covering the full journey to a new delivery model.

Operating model & governance

Choosing between franchising, partnerships and municipal ownership; governance set-up and change management.

Network planning & ticketing

Network design, intermodality, MaaS and fares integration, urban and rural.

Contract design & letting

Contract packaging, cost modelling, procurement and transition to franchised services.

Fleet decarbonisation

Energy choices, depots and hubs, and zero-emission fleet strategy.

Benchmarking & study visits

See franchised networks in France and Germany first-hand and compare with your own.

Proprietary tools

Ready-to-use audit tools for network design and fleet energy choices.
